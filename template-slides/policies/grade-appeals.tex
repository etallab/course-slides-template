\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{Grade Appeals}

  \begin{itemize}
  \item Appeals of the final exam must be within 48 hours.
  \item Other grade appeals are due within one week of the grade's release.
  \item Grade appeals are applicable only to an individual assignment.
  \item Arithmetical errors require no justification.
  \item You must show that I have misapplied the rubric or stated standard.
  \item Appeals may not be resolved in your favor: I reserve the right to reduce your grade in the event of an appeal.
  \end{itemize}
  
\end{frame}
