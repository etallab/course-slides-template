\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{Submission Standards \& Late Work}

  \begin{itemize}
  \item You are responsible for uploading correct, valid (not corrupt, in specified formats) work by the stated deadline.
  \item It is recommended that you use \alert{version control} and \alert{download and verify} your uploaded work.
  \item Only your last upload will be counted.
  \item You will receive two no-questions-asked extensions of 24 hours\\
    (extenuating circumstances must be confirmed by student affairs).
  \item Late work not receiving an extension will be penalized at the rate of a 25\% reduction in grade per day.
  \end{itemize}
  
\end{frame}
