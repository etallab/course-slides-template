\begin{frame}
  \frametitle{Stop/Start/Continue}

  \textbf{Task:} Provide feedback on the course.

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \textbf{Instructions:}
  Chat with folks next to you, try to answer the following questions.
  \begin{description}
  \item[Stop] What instructional elements, things that I am doing as an instructor are hindering your learning?
    Are these things that I should stop?
  \item[Start] Are there things that would be helpful to your learning that I am not doing?
    Things that I should start doing?
    Resources I could provide?
  \item[Continue] What is helping your learning and you would like me to continue?
    How is this helping you?
    Do you want me to do it more?
  \end{description}

  \vspace{0.5em}
  \textbf{After:} Discuss your answers with the class.
  Politely respond to others' comments, add to ideas as you can.

\end{frame}
